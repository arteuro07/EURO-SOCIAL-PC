# BOSS DE RIEN — Hybrid manga visual bible

## Core look
- Hand-drawn manga sensibility with original character designs.
- Rich, controlled color palettes; painterly background depth; clean expressive ink contours.
- Cinematic framing and lighting, with comic-panel rhythm.
- Exaggerated facial acting and readable silhouettes; comedy first, action when the joke benefits.
- Speed lines, impact frames, sweat drops and graphic symbols used intentionally, not on every shot.

## Character continuity
- Kévin: warm brown skin, dense textured afro, charcoal hoodie, white drawstrings, small yellow crown emblem. Proud, theatrical confidence; his eyes and shoulders reveal panic when his plan collapses.
- Junior: blue backward cap, distinctive glasses, blue jacket. Fast-talking pseudo-strategist; sharp gestures and overconfident poses.
- Maya: protective braids, subtle gold jewelry, pink jacket. Calm, observant, sarcastic; controlled expressions that puncture the boys' ego.

## Color and light
- Character colors remain stable between shots.
- Use warm key light and cool fill where it supports depth; backgrounds should not overpower faces.
- Separate line art, base colors, shadows, highlights and FX where possible.
- Keep subtitles and speech lettering inside mobile-safe margins with strong contrast.

## Camera and panels
- Establishing wide shots orient the viewer; medium shots carry acting; close-ups carry punchlines; inserts reveal props and evidence.
- Use low angles for Kévin's false grandeur, high angles for comic defeat, and eye-level for Maya's grounded reactions.
- Camera moves should be motivated: push-in for realization, short shake for impact, pan to reveal a reaction.
- Avoid random zooms and constant movement.

## Animation principles
- Key pose -> anticipation -> action -> follow-through -> settle.
- Use holds for comedic timing. Animate eyes, brows, mouth and hands before adding unnecessary full-body motion.
- Favor strong drawings and clean cuts over lots of weak in-between frames.
- Build mouth shapes for dialogue; sync subtitles to the same timeline.
- Keep motion readable at phone size.

## Originality and approval
All characters, props, dialogue, environments and designs must be original. Do not trace or reproduce existing manga/anime characters, panels, signatures or distinctive franchise designs. A character sheet is canon only after review.
